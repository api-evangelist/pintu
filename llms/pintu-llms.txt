# Pintu

> Pintu (PT Pintu Kemana Saja) is an Indonesian cryptocurrency exchange and investment app (founded 2020, 4M+ users, Bappebti/OJK-licensed). Pintu Pro is its institutional/partner trading platform, exposing an HMAC-signed WebSocket + HTTP order-routing API at docs.pintu.pro with production and sandbox environments.

## APIs

- [Pintu Pro Partner API](https://docs.pintu.pro/): HMAC-SHA256 signed envelope over HTTP and WebSocket; place/cancel orders and stream ExecutionReport, Trade, and user.orders channels. Production wss://partner.pintu.co.id/ws/v1, sandbox wss://partner.sandbox.pintu.co.id/ws/v1.
